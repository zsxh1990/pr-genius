# Small Diff Size

## Pattern

Keep PRs focused and small. Large diffs are harder to review and more likely to have issues.

## Statistics

- < 50 lines: Highest merge rate
- 50-200 lines: Good merge rate
- 200-500 lines: Moderate merge rate
- > 500 lines: Lower merge rate

## Applicability

Universal - all repos
