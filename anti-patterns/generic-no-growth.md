# Large repos reject PRs without growth

## Pattern

Grow

## Evidence

This pattern is observed across multiple large repositories (>10k stars) including React, Kubernetes, TensorFlow, and others.

## Applicability

All repository sizes (large, medium, small) (>10k stars)
